Paul's first and second missionary journeys

Acts 13–18  ·  32 Sermons  ·  Two Journeys  ·  ~2,000 miles

How to Use This

Every city is a moment.
Every moment tells the story.

Between AD 46 and 52, Paul and his team crossed roughly two thousand miles by foot, boat, and Roman road — planting churches, getting stoned, singing at midnight, and turning city after city upside down. This page is a companion to the Acts Journey sermon series, built to help you trace the story geographically, narratively, and theologically.

Three layers, one story. Start anywhere.
