The Living Room Circle

Discussion & Reflection

01

Paul felt something visceral walking into Athens — not polite theological disapproval. When did you last feel genuinely moved by the world around you, the way God is moved? What happened when you did?

02

The two schools in the agora said: the universe doesn't care, so live well — or — there's something bigger, but it won't disturb you. Which of those answers do you find yourself defaulting to, even as a believer?

03

Look back at the territories. Which one is hardest to believe the Resurrection actually changes? Name it honestly. Why do you think that is?

04

Paul carried news, not arguments. Think of a real conversation you've had about faith recently — which one were you doing?

05

Family Prompt: The Resurrection is not Sunday news — it's everyday reality. Where in your family's week this week could you carry the news instead of an argument?
