About This Study

Explore the foundations of the Christian faith.

What do Christians actually believe, and why does it matter? Over 22 weeks, we'll walk through Paul E. Little's classic book Know What You Believe, unpacking the core doctrines of the Christian faith in a way that's accessible, honest, and rooted in Scripture.

Whether you're newer to the faith and want a solid grounding, or you've been a Christian for years and want to revisit the essentials with fresh eyes — this study is for you. Each session is designed for real conversation, not lectures. Come with your questions.

No prior knowledge required. Just bring a Bible, an open mind, and a willingness to learn together.
